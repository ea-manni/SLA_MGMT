SCHEDULE B: SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENTS & REMEDIES

Section 4.1 - Mean Time to Restore (MTTR):
Vendor guarantees that for Tier-1 chiller plant outages, the Mean Time to Restore (MTTR) shall not exceed 4.0 hours per incident. In the event of an MTTR breach, Vendor shall issue a Service Credit equal to 5.0% of the Monthly Maintenance Fee per occurrence.

Section 5.2 - Emergency Response Time:
Vendor's certified technician must arrive and badge in at the IBX facility within 60 minutes of ticket dispatch. Failure to meet the 60-minute window shall incur a Liquidated Penalty of $5,000.00 per breach.

Section 6.3 - Chiller Plant Availability (Uptime):
Vendor commits to maintaining monthly chiller plant uptime of at least 99.98%. Any drop below 99.98% warrants a Service Credit of 10.0% of the Monthly Fee.
